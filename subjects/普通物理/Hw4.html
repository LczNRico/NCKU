Problem 5 / 題目 5
English:
A venturi meter is used to measure the flow speed of a fluid in a pipe. The meter is connected between two sections of the pipe (Fig. 4-6); the cross-sectional area A of the entrance and exit of the meter matches the pipe's cross-sectional area. Between the entrance and exit, the fluid flows from the pipe with speed V and then through a narrow "throat" of cross-sectional area a with speed v. A manometer connects the wider portion of the meter to the narrower portion. The change in the fluid's speed is accompanied by a change Δp in the fluid's pressure, which causes a height difference h of the liquid in the two arms of the manometer. (Here Δp means pressure in the throat minus pressure in the pipe.) (a) By applying Bernoulli's equation and the equation of continuity to points 1 and 2 in Fig. 4-6, show that

V = √[2a²Δp / ρ(a² - A²)]

where ρ is the density of the fluid. (b) Suppose that the fluid is fresh water, that the cross-sectional areas are 60 cm² in the pipe and 32 cm² in the throat, and that the pressure is 55 kPa in the pipe and 41 kPa in the throat. What is the rate of water flow in cubic meters per second?
中文翻譯：
文丘里計用於測量管道中流體的流速。該儀表連接在管道的兩段之間（圖 4-6）；儀表入口和出口的截面積 A 與管道的截面積相同。在入口和出口之間，流體以速度 V 從管道流出，然後通過截面積為 a 的狹窄「喉部」，速度為 v。壓力計連接儀表的較寬部分和較窄部分。流體速度的變化伴隨著流體壓力變化 Δp，這導致壓力計兩臂中液體的高度差 h。（這裡 Δp 表示喉部壓力減去管道壓力。）(a) 將伯努利方程和連續性方程應用於圖 4-6 中的點 1 和 2，證明：

V = √[2a²Δp / ρ(a² - A²)]

其中 ρ 是流體的密度。(b) 假設流體是淡水，管道中的截面積為 60 cm²，喉部為 32 cm²，管道中的壓力為 55 kPa，喉部為 41 kPa。水的流速是每秒多少立方米？
